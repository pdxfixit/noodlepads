One-line control bars

Phones only. Each bar keeps its controls on one line by giving up words first (🎬 Takes → 🎬, ● Record → ●), and the tempo becomes one key, the number, that opens the tempo when tapped. On a wider screen nothing changes: the tempo slider stays in the bar. Every key stays at least 44 px wide and 52 px tall.

🎤 Live

Now: two lines. Proposed: one line from 360 px. ● Record keeps its word where it fits. At 320 px six 44 px keys need about 284 px and the bar has 264, so the narrowest phones (an old iPhone SE) keep two lines.

Tapping the tempo: three ways it could open

Either way: drag the slider, or ⌄ ⌃ for the next common tempo, or tap the number to type one. The tempo changes as you go (the click and a playing beat follow at once). Tapping outside, the tempo key again or Esc closes it.

The closed tempo key

Left: as drawn above, with the word BPM under the number. Right: the number and a ▾ that says it opens, no music word (screen readers still hear "Tempo 100 BPM").
