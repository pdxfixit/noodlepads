Beat Rows Mockup

Beat rows with the controls on the left

A mockup of every beat row in the app with its controls in a column on the left, its pills on one line, and the grid scrolling sideways. It uses the real 🍭 Pop groove (8 rows, 4 bars). It has no sound. On a phone: the emoji opens the row's menu and 🔊 mutes. Wide: every button shows. ▶ Play follows the playhead a screen at a time. Scroll it yourself and it stops following until the next Play.

Try this

  • Tap a pill to turn it on. Swipe sideways across the pills: the grid scrolls and nothing turns on.
  • At 375 px, tap an emoji: the menu holds 🔄 Change sound, 🔔 Overlap, 🧹 Clear, ✕ Remove and ↑ ↓ to move the row.
  • Press ▶ Play, then swipe the grid: "Following" turns off until you press Play again.
  • Switch 1, 2 or 4 bars, and the widths, and compare "Time on screen".

What changes from today

  • Each row is one line, at every width. Today a 4-bar row on a phone wraps into 8 lines of pills.
  • ▾ Fold goes. A row that's one line has nothing to fold.
  • The ⠿ grip stays on wide screens. On a phone, ↑ ↓ are in the menu.
  • ⌨️ Keys on and off use the same rows. 🎹 Piano roll and 🎼 Song use the same left column and the same sideways scroll.
  • "Nothing scrolls sideways" (decisions.md) gets an exception for these grids, as the Mixer has.
