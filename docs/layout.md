# Layout

Where everything is: one line per file or folder. Each module in `web/` also
opens with a comment saying what it owns. How the app behaves is in
[guide.md](guide.md).

- `web/`: the app (plain HTML, CSS and ES modules, no build step)
  - `app.js`: starts everything; who is showing, Edit sounds, the bottom bar's two modes, 🎉 Play (Pads, Live) and 🛠️ Make (Beats, Song) and Beats' ⌨️ Keys
  - `dj.js`, `rhythm.js`: the keys (like a periodic table), on Live and on Beats with ⌨️ Keys, and its pure rules: keymap, default keys, layouts, the forgiving snap
  - `dj-keys.js`, `keyset.js`: ✏️ Layout with ⌨️ Keys, and its pure rules: placing sounds, moving what was in the way, swaps, rows, the ready-made key layouts
  - `dj-touch.js`, `touch.js`: 👆 the touch layout (ADR 0029): drawing its bands for playing and ✏️ Layout with 👆 Pads, and its pure rules: bands of sounds, their sizes and starting sounds, the ready-made ones, editing, cleaning; `touch-grid.js`: a layout's grid (ADR 0032), the tree of splits placing its bands in blocks side by side: the ready-made grids, filling and emptying blocks, edges, splitting and joining, cleaning, and how many pad columns fill a block
  - data: `store.js` (IndexedDB schema and upgrades), `state.js` (what is in memory, and saving it)
  - the board: `model.js` (pure rules: people, clip order, edits), `board.js` (those, bound to the state)
  - sound: `audio.js` (playing, looping, caching voiced clips), `meter.js` (the speaker grille)
  - screens: `pads.js` (its size slider's maths in `pad-size.js`; a kid's own groups' rules in `groups.js`), `bulk.js` (Edit sounds with sounds selected: the bar, its sheets, Undo), `sheet.js` (new/edit sound), `wave.js` (the sound's wave in the sheet), `shape.js` (the sheet's Shape it cards, Advanced), `chop.js` (✂️ trim, the wave editor's fades and part edits, and 🧩 chop on the wave; the pure parts, onsets, marks, edits and fades, are in `cuts.js`; how loud a sound seems, for 🔊 Even it out, in `loudness.js`), `people.js`, `things.js` (🗂️ See all: everything of one person's, from a profile's count line, each row playing in place), `backup-ui.js`, `settings-ui.js` (the ⚙️ Settings dialog; each setting's default and allowed values are declared in `settings.js`), `leftover.js` (Settings → Older sounds found on this device), `help.js` (the ❓ picture reference), `kids.js` (🧸 Kids' screens; pure parts in `kids-logic.js`), `grown-up.js` (the grown-up sum, asked by 🧸 Kids' ⚙️ and by Feedback), `feedback.js` (⚙️ Settings → About → Feedback: the dialog and the sending to `/api/feedback`), `dialogs.js` (closing, focus, and who hears of a dialog opening), `ui.js` (element lookup, the banner over the top of the screen for every notice and Undo, fading, the slim header)
  - `voices.js`: the voice effects (Web Audio `OfflineAudioContext`); `steps.js`: the other building blocks of a chain (echo, room, Muffled ↔ Bright…), live or rendered; `stretch.js` is Fast and Slow's pitch-keeping stretch, run off the page in `stretch-worker.js`; `tweaks.js`: the sound sheet's Knobs, as steps; `voice-knobs.js`: voices as knob settings (stacking, the line under the voices, ⤢ Turn into knobs, a kid's own voices)
  - `sequencer.js`: the beat maker screen (16-step rows, scheduled on the audio clock); `beat-engine.js`: the engine under it (the sounds loaded for scheduling, one hit, the mix graphs and the "play a saved beat" path that 🎼 Song and the share preview book through; made once by `app.js`; its `play` is the one scheduler on the audio clock: a loop of a beat with hooks (🥁 Beats, 🧸 Kids) or a planned list (🎼 Song, 💃 Grooves' sample)); `beat.js`: its pure rules (a beat made safe, the hits it plans and the hits due (`hitsDue`), swing, grooves as beats, beat files, tempo stops; no DOM, storage or audio), which the backup gate, 🧸 Kids' rules, the listener, 🎼 Song and the share preview load without the screen; `wav.js`: the 16-bit WAV encoder and the render normalizer (`encodeWav`, `normalize`), and the rules of ⬇️ Save as audio file's file (passes to 15 s, ring-out kept, name); `old-beats.js`: the time signatures, and the one-time stamp for beats saved before each had its own (the store upgrade, backups, beat files); `grooves.js`: the 💃 Grooves library, by style; `beat-row.js`: a beat row's emoji and its 🔔 · 🔊 · 🧹 · ✕, shared by Beats with ⌨️ Keys off and on (on Beats the emoji opens the row's menu, which holds all but 🔊); `row-volume.js`: a row's speaker (tap mutes, hold or drag sets its loudness); `mixer.js`: the 🎚️ Mixer sheet, its effects and the mix graph; `notes.js`: a tune row's notes (placing, stretching, the scale lock, old melody rows); `roll.js`: 🎹 the piano roll screen; `slide.js`: the pure rules of a pick and a slide round the beat; `pill-select.js`: lit pills picked and dragged like objects with a mouse (click, Ctrl/Cmd, Shift, a box, Delete, double-click), and with a finger while ⇆ locks the pills, on Beats' rows and the ⌨️ Keys grid; `mini-deck.js`: the play bar tucked into the slim top bar once scrolled (▶ ● ✏️ ▾), and what gives way there as room runs out; `edge-scroll.js`: a drag near the top or bottom of the screen scrolls the page (a row's grip ⠿, ⇆ Move); `follow.js`: a box that scrolls sideways follows the playhead a page at a time until the kid scrolls it (Beats' rows, the piano roll, ⌨️ Keys' grid; 🎼 Song's lanes use its `follower` and work out their own page turn in `song.js`)
  - `listen-input.js`: 🎧 From a song's inputs (docs/design/beat-from-a-song.md): which ways to listen a device offers (`sources()`: another tab, the whole computer, the microphone, a song file), capturing each as mono frames (`startCapture`, `readFile`, capped at 20 MB and 15 minutes), and the plain sentences for what went wrong; `listen-worklet.js`: the AudioWorklet that batches what it hears into frames of 2048 samples, and the mono mixing and batching it shares with `readFile`
  - `song.js`: 🎼 Song: the playlist screen, playing it (a run of the beat engine on the planned list), Save as a sound; `playlist.js`: its pure rules (lanes, placing blocks without overlap, repeats, time at the song tempo, old songs' slots, a stored song cleaned and linked, a song planned to play and sounded offline)
  - `backup.js`, `zip.js`: backups to and from a zip; `merge.js`: loading one merges into the device
  - `old-backup.js`: 💾 Save everything it left, for Older sounds found on this device; `starters.js`: the old app's starter sounds, which are presets now
  - `util.js`, `contrast.js`, `download.js`: small shared helpers; color contrast math; handing a file to the user
  - `version.js`: `RELEASE`, the release (`1.0.<commit count>`; `just version` bumps it); the deploy labels `version.json` from it (`site/site.conf` `release=`). The page does not load it (`NOT_LOADED` in `tests/offline.test.mjs`)
  - `backdrop.js`: the faint pattern behind the panels, one of the tiles in `bg/` picked at random each visit and drawn as a mask in the casing color; nothing remembered (`?bg=<name>` or `none` asks for one, for `just look --bg`)
  - `tips.js`: one-time tips in the banner and hint dots on ⌨️ Keys, 💃 Grooves and 🎚️ Mixer, whether 🧸 Kids' 👆 hand and mic wiggle still show (ADR 0028), what a person has seen of them (the `seen` setting), and ❓ Help's 💡 Show tips again
  - `nudge.js`: 🎛️ Want more knobs?, the level-up nudge for Regular people (ADR 0028): counting reaches for more (the `reaches` setting), when to offer it, its bar in the update bar's place
  - `welcome.js`: 👋 the welcome for a new person (ADR 0028): Who's playing? (three picture pads set the level), then Pick your stage name, and who counts as new; `who-now.js`: 👋 Who's playing now?, the chooser the board starts over on (after the person on show is deleted, and at start when everyone has a PIN); `firsts.js`: the pure parts of a new person's first steps, and the test hook that turns them off
  - `skins.js`: the pad skins (Tiles, Slam buttons, Lit pads, Switch, Spinner, Jelly, Googly eyes): the list, the next one round, and `showSkin`, which puts the person's `padSkin` on the page's root as `data-skin`; `styles.css` ("Pad skins") draws them
  - `tooltips.js`: a tooltip on every control, from its `aria-label` or text, unless it has a `title` of its own
  - `contact.js`: the PIN-reset address, built from an encoded constant so it is never written out in a served or tracked file
  - `too-old.js`: the browser floor: a classic script, run before `app.js`, that checks a few of the newest features the app needs and, when one is missing, shows "This browser is too old" and marks the page so `app.js` does not start
  - `boot.js`: the reload guard: a classic script, run before `app.js`, that reloads the page once when a module fails to load because two versions of the app met across a deploy (it asks `version.json`, and a `sessionStorage` flag keeps it from ever looping)
  - `update.js`: the "🆕 An update for Noodle Pads is ready." bar (when to ask `version.json`, when to show)
  - `sw.js`: the service worker: the whole app kept for opening with no network (`SHELL`, every file the app can load; `tests/offline.test.mjs` fails when one is missing), network first, `/api/` untouched, `version.json` network only; `offline.js` registers it once the page has loaded
  - `manifest.webmanifest`, `app-icons/`: installing it as an app (name, colors, the 192 and 512 px icons, a maskable 512 and the 180 px `apple-touch-icon`, drawn from the tab icon). Not `icons/`: the global git ignore's `Icon?` (macOS folder icons) matches it on a Mac's case-insensitive disk, and its files would silently stay out of a commit
  - `log.js` (the in-memory event log), `log-clean.js` (what a problem report may hold), `log-send.js` (sending it), `tap-watch.js` (a finger tap on a pad, key or tile that played nothing, logged with how far it got), `agent.js` (a User-Agent read as browser, OS and device-type families; a report sends only these, and the Worker's `metrics.js` uses it too)
  - `device-meta.js`: what the device says about itself on each cloud call (`X-Noodle-App`: version, width bucket, touch), for the Worker's `devices` and `activity` (ADR 0016); the Worker imports `WIDTHS` from it
  - `play-count.js`: pads, keys, roll notes, song blocks and Kids pads played this visit, one count each, in memory; `track-page.js` sends them as `noodlepads.play` as the page hides
  - `telemetry.js`: usage events in the shared standard (ADR 0026): `track(event, details)` checks each event against `EVENTS` and hands it to `events-client.js`; the player id (`telemetry-player`); `shouldSend` (the switch; every device sends, question #425)
  - `events-client.js`: flarepress's shared events client, vendored (never edited here): fills each record's columns, queues it in localStorage `events:noodlepads:<session>`, sends batches to `/api/events`
  - `track-page.js`: where the events come from: lifecycle, screens, dialogs, one click listener for every control (named without a kid's words), content, settings, errors and speed; the browser family on `app.open`, `to` on `screen.view`, and `noodlepads.level` once a load
  - `usage.js`: the usage switch (`sendUsage`) as the counting modules read it; imports nothing, so the Worker can import them
  - `overflow.js`: a row's menu (`moreMenu`): on Beats and ⌨️ Keys a row's emoji opens it, listing the row's buttons marked `data-more-word` (🔄 Change sound or 🖼️ Change picture, 🔔 🧹 ✕, and ↑ ↓ on Beats) as icon + word; a tap there taps the real button. Its ⋯ for a line that runs out of room (`fitMore`, the `more-tight` class) is used by no screen today
  - `lines.js`: lines of controls that stay on one line, giving way step by step as room runs out (the toolbar, the beat bar, the beat's bars · time signature · swing), and keys for a slider with five stops or fewer (Tone, On the beat's note lengths), falling back to the slider when they don't fit
  - `tempo-key.js`: on a phone, each control bar's tempo folded into one key (the number over ▾) that opens it as a line of its own under the bar; Beats, ⌨️ Keys, 🎤 Live's click and 🎼 Song each keep their own tempo controls inside it
  - `mix.js`: ➕ Mix with another sound (pure: two sounds in, one out)
  - `pitch.js`: 🎵 Find its note (pure: samples in, a note out)
  - `synth.js`: ✨ Make one's sounds from nothing (pure: recipe in, samples out)
  - `listen-ui.js`: 🎧 From a song's screens: the card in 💃 Grooves (never for 🧸 Kids), and one sheet: what to listen to, listening live (rolling bars drawn from `snapshot()`, only the cells that change), what it heard (▶ each part, 🎼 Open it as a song, 🥁 Open part A, 🗑️ Throw away) and no beat heard; it saves the beats and the song through the app's own paths, and `listenEnv` is its test hook; `listen-make.js`: its pure parts (what a snapshot shows, the closest sounds, the beats and song made from `finish()`, the words of What I heard)
  - `listen.js`, `listen-dsp.js`: 🎧 From a song's listener (pure: samples in; tempo, time signature, swing, parts and each part's kick, snare and hat out, live as `snapshot()` and at the end as `finish()`; its opening comment is the contract the screens build on), and its signal processing (kick, snare and hat hits from the spectrum, a sound's fingerprint, the closest sound)
  - `emoji-more.js`: the "…" at the end of every picture chooser (all emoji, grouped, with search); `emoji-all.js` (the list with names, loaded on the first tap of …) and `emoji-check.js` (`isListedEmoji`, for backups and the Worker) are made by `tools/make_emoji.py` (`just emoji`) from Python's Unicode data. Do not edit them by hand
  - `names.js`: the made-up default names ("Wobbly Thunder" for a beat; "Admiral Avocado", a person's fun name; `keptName`, a kept groove's "<fun name> (<groove> version)"); `scratch.js`: the scratch beat and song's reserved ids, and 🧸 Kids' old beat becoming the scratch (ADR 0020)
  - `reid.js`: random person ids, and the one rule that renames an old plain id everywhere on the device
  - `cloud.js`, `cloud-sync.js`, `cloud-api.js`, `cloud-ui.js`: cloud profiles on the device (binding it to storage, the rules, the routes, the screens); `sync-timing.js`: when it syncs; `merge3.js`: the three-way merge of one record; `share-render.js`: a share's preview, recorded as it plays (also 🗂️ See all's beats, songs and takes, and a take made into a sound); `privacy.js`: README's "What's private" lines (the app links the shared policy instead)
  - `fonts/`: the app's two fonts, served from this site: `fonts.css` (Google Fonts' faces, pointed here), the WOFF2 subsets of Anybody and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, and their licenses (SIL OFL 1.1)
  - `boards.json`: the kit, its groups, the palette, and people (none today). v2 reads this format too.
  - `instruments.js`: who plays Notes, Chords and Bass (ADR 0030): the instruments by family, the person's picks (settings `instNotes`, `instChords`, `instBass`), which presets a pick shows, and the sum that plays one recording as any note or chord (pure); `inst-picker.js`: the 🎹 Piano ▾ picker on those groups' headers (🔊 Pads, the beat sound chooser, ✏️ Edit keys, 👆 bands, the piano roll, 🎹 Add a tune's Who plays it?)
- `worker/`: the Worker script beside the static files, with one D1 database and one R2 bucket for usage events (`docs/deploy-cloudflare.md`); `schema.sql` is the full schema, `migrations/` brings the live one there (`just cloud-migrate`)
  - `index.js`: routes `/api/log` to `reports.js`, `/api/events` and `/api/feedback` to `events-worker.js`, the rest of `/api/` to `cloud.js`; every other path goes to the static files
  - `events-worker.js`: flarepress's shared `/api/events` route, vendored (`just events-vendor` in flarepress; never edited here): checks the origin, size and batch id, stores the batch in R2 (`EVENTS`) as sent; and its `/api/feedback` route (`handleFeedback`): the same checks plus a rate limit (`FEEDBACK_LIMITER`), stores what was typed in the shared R2 bucket (`FEEDBACK`, `app-suggestions`) under `v1/noodlepads/`
  - `cloud.js`: cloud profiles: the list, keys, records by rev, sounds in 1.9 MB pieces, share codes, last activity, free-plan caps, per-profile rate limits
  - `metrics.js`: player metrics (ADR 0016): each device's row and the activity log with its place (nothing of the IP), the User-Agent read as families; 14 days kept (a device's row 90); skipped while its tables are missing. The feature-use counts and visits it once kept are retired (ADR 0027)
  - `pin.js`: PINs (salted PBKDF2, 5 tries then a 10-minute wait) and share codes; `http.js`: shared answers, ids, daily counters
  - `reports.js`: `POST /api/log` takes a problem report (site's Origin, JSON, 64 KB, 10 a minute per device, 1,000 routine and 1,000 with an error or silent tap a day) into D1 for 14 days
  - `clean.js`: the Worker's check of every problem report: it runs the app's own rules again (`web/log-clean.js cleanPayload`: numbers, booleans, listed values, the kit's sound ids in `KIT_IDS`, and seven free-text fields, every known name and quote blanked: an error's `message` and `storage`, a notice's `text`, a backup's and a sync's `error`, a silent tap's `over` and `dialog`)
  - `schema.sql`: the D1 tables
- `tools/logs.py`: `just logs`, problem reports read from the telemetry runner's mirror (`noodlepads__reports` in `~/.local/state/telemetry/telemetry.sqlite3`, read only): list, `--errors`, a device code, `--json`, `--grep`, `--sql`, and the weekly `--digest`. `~/.config/noodlepads/cloud.json` (outside the repo; template `tools/cloud.example.json`, read by `tools/cloud_config.py`) names the D1 account and database for the cloud-db recipes. Tests: `tools/test_logs.py`, `tools/test_cloud_config.py`
- `tools/runner_export.py`: `just runner-export`, once: the old Mac archives (`logs.sqlite3`, `metrics.sqlite3`) in the harvest's own shape, both in one file (`all`) for a single `telemetry.py import`, and `check` (docs/how-to.md, "Retire the old Mac archives"). Tests: `tools/test_runner_export.py`
- `tools/cloud_admin.py`: the cloud database's chores, behind `just cloud-schema`, `cloud-migrate`, `cloud-profiles`, `cloud-stats` (reports from the telemetry runner's pulled store, read only), `cloud-prune` and `cloud-reset-pin` (by fun name or id; a new random PIN, shown once; dry run unless `--yes`; through `just cloud-db`). Tests: `tools/test_cloud_admin.py`
- `tools/serve.py`: dev server with caching off; sends the same headers as the live site (`web/_headers`, the Content-Security-Policy included)
- `tools/ensure_ip_key.sh`: gives the Worker its `IP_HASH_KEY` secret (random, by wrangler from stdin) when it has none; the deploy runs it before the upload. Tests: `tests/worker-ip-limit.test.mjs`
- `tests/`: `just test` (Node's built-in runner, no install). `fake-dom.mjs` builds the page from `index.html` so `app.test.mjs` can start the real app, on `test-board.mjs`'s made-up people (`fresh-start.test.mjs` uses the real boards.json); `banner.mjs` asks whether the banner offers Undo, and stands in for it where a test has no page; `listen-synth.mjs` makes up songs (drums over a bass line, a pad and hiss) for `listen.test.mjs`; `fake-idb.mjs` and `fake-d1.mjs` stand in for IndexedDB and the cloud's D1 (on Node's SQLite); `new-beat.mjs` makes a named beat with a row and turns ⌨️ Keys on and off; `song-ui.mjs` drives 🎼 Song's strip and lanes; `helpers.mjs` has odd zips, fake audio buffers and an offline renderer; `fixtures/` holds the golden backups (`backup-1.0.zip`, `backup-1.1.zip`, each with its `.expected.json`, made once by `make-golden-backups.mjs` and never regenerated) that `golden-backups.test.mjs` loads; `formats-doc.test.mjs` checks `docs/formats.md` names every store and field; `unended-reporter.mjs` is `just test`'s second reporter, the files a cut run left a test unreported in; there is no browser in the tests, so sound, recording, IndexedDB and layout are checked by hand
- `tools/make_placeholders.py`: synthesizes the presets' clips, the struck and plucked instruments' notes included (marimba, steel drum, kalimba, chip, guitar, 808); `just placeholders quack roar` makes only those, so the others stay byte-identical. Tests: `tools/test_make_placeholders.py`
- `tools/preset_loudness.py`: how loud each preset seems as the app plays it on a phone, from the middle (`just preset-loudness`); `--fix` writes the boards.json `level` that turns each one over the middle down to it. Tests: `tools/test_preset_loudness.py` (fails until the levels match)
- `tools/make_samples.py`: the recorded instruments' notes (piano, strings, brass, flute, organ, upright bass) from the CC0 libraries VSCO 2 CE and VCSL, and the real drums, animals and effects (`HITS`) from VCSL and ESC-50, cut, faded and made mono 22050 Hz like the kit; the libraries stay outside the repo (`web/clips/kit/LICENSE-samples.txt` says what came from where). `lift` raises a recorded hit's body under the same peak (a limiter), so a real drum plays as loud as a made one. Run with `python3 -I`. Tests: `tools/test_make_samples.py`
- `tools/kit_instruments.py`: writes every instrument's presets into `boards.json` (ADR 0030): each of Notes, Chords and Bass's own presets again, played by the instrument's nearest recording moved to its note (`shift`) or as a chord (`chord`). Tests: `tools/test_kit_instruments.py`
- `tools/cloud_config.py`: where this Mac keeps Noodle Pads' own things outside the repo: the Cloudflare ids (`~/.config/noodlepads/cloud.json`, template `tools/cloud.example.json`), the archives' folder (`~/.local/state/noodlepads`) and the Keychain token's name. Tests: `tools/test_cloud_config.py`. `tools/test_justfile.py` checks every recipe has a doc line and is named in "Running it yourself"
- `site/`: the site folder flarepress deploys: `wrangler.toml` (assets `../web`, Worker `../worker/index.js`, the D1 id as `${D1_DATABASE_ID}`, the R2 bucket `noodlepads-telemetry` as `EVENTS`), `site.conf` (the shared deploy steps' settings), `harvest.conf` (what the harvest copies). `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` deploys every push to `main` with it (ADR 0025)
- `tools/live_check.py`: `just live-check`, the daily live check GitHub runs (`.github/workflows/live.yml`): the site answers and `/api/cloud` says ok (3 retries, 20 s apart); it does not check which commit is live. Read-only. Tests: `tools/test_live_check.py`
- `tools/council.py`: `just council-tripwire [N]`, the council's drift check: reads the `Council:` trailers in git and names any seat whose last N reviews were all a plain ✅ (docs/council.md). Tests: `tools/test_council.py`
- `tools/node_tests.sh`: the Node half of `just test`: `node --test` with the spec reporter and `tests/unended-reporter.mjs`; a file `--test-force-exit` cut short ("▶") runs again alone, and its exit code counts. Tests: `tools/test_node_tests.py`
- `tools/doctor.sh`: `just doctor`, a fresh session's first command: this checkout (main or a worktree), just, node, python, Chrome, the public flarepress clone, live (version.json's commit) against `main`, then the reading order. Tests: `tools/test_doctor.py`
- `land.conf`: `just land`'s settings (flarepress's shared `tools/land.sh`, run from the flarepress checkout beside this one): the test it runs (with `tools/version_bumped.sh`), the restamp after each pick, and the files whose conflicts its resolvers settle
- `tools/bump_version.py`: `just version`, the release in `web/version.js` (PATCH is the commit count; `minor`, `major`, `--show`; `--check` for CI and `just ship`, `--restamp` for `just land`). `tools/version_bumped.sh` fails when `web/` changed since the last bump (CI, `just ship`, `just land`). Tests: `tools/test_bump_version.py`
- `tools/make_emoji.py`: `just emoji` (see `emoji-more.js` above). Tests: `tools/test_make_emoji.py`
- `tools/look.mjs`: `just look`, the five-width check (nine with `--wide`, up to 3440 px) in a private headless Chrome (sideways scroll, errors, CSP, controls under 44 px tall or wide, controls overlapping in a fixed or sticky bar, a page with no controls, screenshots), on any screen or named view (`settings`, `sheet`, `builder`…), or a page of the site beside the app (a view's `page`); `--bg NAME,…|all|none` sets the page's pattern, one shot each (a contact sheet with `all`)
  - `tools/chrome.mjs` starts each run's private headless Chrome (the Mac's, or a cloud session's Chromium, unsandboxed as root); `tools/look-cloud.mjs` holds the live checks `just cloud-smoke` and `just cloud-e2e`; `look.mjs taps` is `just taps` (a finger on a phone: every tap must sound). With `look.mjs`, these are the only Node tools
- `skills/noodlepads-logs/`: the recurrent routine's `noodlepads-logs` leg (weekly `just logs --digest`; it copies nothing), kept here until it moves to `~/src/skills` (TODO.md); `just test` runs its tests
- `docs/formats.md`: the backup format (1.2), field by field, for v2's importer and anyone reading a backup with no JavaScript; golden samples in `tests/fixtures/`
- `docs/deploy-cloudflare.md`: hosting at noodlepads.com; `docs/how-to.md`: step by step for the common jobs
- `docs/architecture/`: how the code is wired, one file per area (README.md is the index and the "one or several?" table); `tests/architecture.test.mjs` checks it against the code
- `web/_headers`, `web/.assetsignore`: response headers and upload exclusions for Cloudflare
