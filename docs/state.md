# State

Where things stand **now**. Present tense only: replace lines, never append history (`git log` holds that). Update it in the same commit whenever what is live or in flight changes.

## Live

- The app is Noodle Pads at https://noodlepads.com/ (ADR 0010), served by one Worker (`worker/`, the `/api` routes) and one D1 database, `noodle-pads` (ADR 0002). The old address is retired (ADR 0010, 2026-10-13): the move page and the app's receiving end are gone, and its Worker and flarepress folder are deleted. Manage the database with `just cloud-migrate`, `cloud-profiles`, `cloud-prune`, `cloud-reset-pin`, `cloud-sql`, `cloud-backup` and `cloud-db` (README "Running it yourself" lists every recipe).
- Release 1.0 (CHANGELOG.md): the corner and About say `1.0 · <commit>`. The name is `noodlepads` in backups, beat files and downloads (older `noodle-pads` files still load); the browser database and the D1 database keep `noodle-pads`. The Cloudflare ids live in `~/.config/noodlepads/cloud.json`, not the repo.
- noodlepads.com serves `main`: its `version.json` names the commit (`just deploy-verify` checks it; `just live` and `just doctor` compare it with `main`). The shared deploy steps come from flarepress's `tools/site.sh` (`noodlepads.com/site.conf`). GitHub runs `just test` on every push (`.github/workflows/test.yml`) and checks the live site daily (`just live-check`, `.github/workflows/live.yml`).
- Every commit to `main` is deployed (`just --yes deploy`), then checked live: `just cloud-smoke` (the API) and `just cloud-e2e` (two devices syncing). `just cloud-prune --smoke` clears what a killed check left.

## Built

- Four peer tabs (ADR 0017, 2026-10-12): 🔊 Pads · 🥁 Beats · 🎼 Song · 🎤 Live, then Stop all (⏹️ alone on a phone); no screen has a sub-switch. Beats has ⌨️ Keys in its beat bar (remembered per device): the keys under the open beat, with ● Record, as DJ's Beat Builder had. 🎼 Song (a list of songs, each a playlist) is for everyone. 🎤 Live is Freestyle: keys, takes, ⏲️ and ✏️ Edit keys with saved key layouts (ADR 0013). Not yet tried on a phone.
- ❓ Help, Chop and trim, the wave editor (✂️ Trim and edit: Ends, Fades, Select part), and sound design (recording helpers, Shape it with live sliders, ⤢, ✨ Make one, 🎵 Find its note, ➕ Mix with). The app is called Noodle Pads; the person is a round button in the top right.
- The UX review is done: name bar → settings deck → rows on Beats (⌨️ Keys off and on) and Song, and lines of controls give way in order (`web/lines.js`, rules in `docs/decisions.md` → Screens).
- Wide screens fill, then split: content up to 2400 px; from 1400 px Beats with ⌨️ Keys, ❓ Help and the sound sheet put their two parts side by side (end of `web/styles.css`). The pad size slider runs from this screen's smallest pads to its biggest (`web/pad-size.js`).
- The page behind the panels has a faint pattern (2026-10-13): one of 23 SVG tiles (`web/bg/`, `web/backdrop.js`), picked at random each visit, in the person's color lightened 10%, behind 🧸 Kids too; nothing remembered. Panels stay solid; the sticky bars (slim header, Pads' slider bar, toolbar) carry the pattern lined up with the page's, over their solid color.
- Each person has one open beat (Beats, ⌨️ Keys on or off) and one open song (ADR 0008, ADR 0017). With nothing else open, it is their scratch beat or scratch song (ADR 0020, 2026-10-12): never listed, saved once it changes, synced and backed up as an ordinary record (`beat-scratch-<person>`, `song-scratch-<person>`); 💾 Keep turns it into a named one in 🗂️ My beats or 🗂️ My songs. A groove loads into the scratch, Keys off or on. 🧸 Kids' beat is the scratch; an old kids beat is adopted. Not yet tried on a phone. An older device's Beat Builder beat stays in 🗂️ My beats; its deck drops the old `beatId` when it next saves.
- 📂 Open is 🗂️ My beats in the beat bar (just 🗂️ under 560 px) and 🗂️ My songs on Song (Ben, 2026-10-12), dialog titles too.

- Backups are format 1.1 (`noodlepads`, and the older `noodle-pads` still loads; 1.0 plus groups); backups from before 1.0 (the testing copies) still load through the old-store conversions (the same as Older sounds found on this device), until launch. Loading a backup, or older sounds, merges by id and says what came; an empty default first person is dropped (ADR 0011).
- A page left open says "🆕 An update for Noodle Pads is ready." with Reload once a newer version is live (`web/update.js`); it waits while anything records, plays or is open.
- 🧸 Kids (ADR 0019, 2026-10-12): each person has a level, 🧸 Kids · Regular · Advanced (Settings → Level; stored as `level` beside `advancedBeats`). Kids shows four picture screens (`web/kids.js`): pages of big pads (swipe, pinch), a hold-to-record mic with 🤖 🐭 👻, a 4 × 8 beat saved in the library, eight toy keys, and ⏹️; ⚙️ asks a grown-up sum. Not yet tried on a phone or by a five-year-old.
- 👥 People is a short list, one line per person (picture, name, counts, ☁️ and 🔒); a tap opens their profile sheet: 🎲 Pick a name, picture, every color, ☁️ Cloud, 🔒 PIN, Delete (2026-10-12). With a PIN, a change to the name, picture or color asks for it once per visit (`cloud.js` `pinGuards`); syncs are not gated. Not yet tried on a phone.
- ⚙️ Advanced shows 20 more presets, the sharps and flats (Notes, Chords, Bass; `"advanced": true` in boards.json); off, they hide, and anything using one keeps playing it. Not yet listened to.
- Beats with ⌨️ Keys: one tempo by ● Record, live while recording after the count-in; beats grow 1 → 2 → 4 bars while played into; tap tempo on ⏲️ (`docs/design/beat-builder-tempo-bars.md`, Option A). Not yet tried on a phone for feel.
- One tap turns a pill on or off, a hold makes it loud; with ⌨️ Keys a beat removes rows and holds 16; a beat moves between copies as a `.beat.json` file; takes stop; the footer how-tos are worded for touch or mouse.
- Each beat row has a speaker on Beats, ⌨️ Keys off and on: tap mutes, hold or drag sets its loudness (row `volume`, ADR 0015). Not yet heard on a phone.
- One beat panel (2026-10-12): Beats with ⌨️ Keys off and on share the title bar (🥁 name ✏️ or 💾 · 💃 · ⌨️ · 🆕 · 🗂️), the control bar (▶ Play, ● Record with Keys on, tempo, ⏲️, bars; `.beat-deck`) and the row parts (`web/beat-row.js`). With Keys off a row starts with one header line (⠿ ▾ emoji … 🔔 🔊 🧹 ✕, every button 44 px; under 390 px the name hides, and where the line is short of room, under about 360 px, 🔔 🧹 ✕ move into a ⋯ menu at its end, `web/overflow.js`, the app-wide helper) and its pills fill the width below; collapsed, it is just that line. With Keys on, compact one-line rows. A row's 🔔 🔊 🧹 ✕ and ⋯ look alike: one outlined square, `.icon-btn` (Song's lane speakers too), lit with the app's yellow when on (🔔 on, 🔊 muted), red while asking; 🔊's loudness is a bar along its bottom; the compact rows scale the same squares to 26 px. The mixer's Mute lights yellow as Solo does.
- ✏️ Edit sounds: ✏️ and 🗑️ on each pad (✏️ on hover with a mouse while playing too), tapping pads selects them for Move to group, Color · picture, Voice, Share (one code) and Delete with Undo, and a kid's own sounds can be in groups of their own (ADR 0013). Backups are format 1.1.

- 💃 Grooves (2026-10-12): 33 grooves in six style groups (`web/grooves.js`), many with bass and chords, in the beat bar; each has a ▶ sample (one pass, at most 4 s) in the chooser. A groove loads into the scratch beat; with ⌨️ Keys on it loads stopped, ▶ Play loops it behind you, ● Record adds your hits, and ↩️ My beat undoes the load. Not yet listened to on a phone.
- Pattern chips (2026-10-12): under the beat bar on Beats (⌨️ Keys off and on), 8 numbered spots: beats pinned with 📌 in 🗂️ My beats first (a `pinned` time on the beat record, ADR 0021, proposed), then the most recently used, oldest made first, so a tap never moves a chip; no label yet; a tap opens one, and while playing it switches at the next bar line without stopping. Locked while ● Record records. Not yet tried on a phone.
- 🎚️ Mixer (2026-10-12): after the bars on the control bar (⌨️ Keys off and on), a sheet with a column per row (fader = the row's loudness, Mute, Solo, one effect) and a Master (loudness, effect). Solo, effects and the master save with the beat (ADR 0018, proposed); compact rows dim when soloed out and ring a soloed emoji. Every hit plays through the mix graph (`web/mixer.js`), live, in Save as a sound, songs and share previews. Not yet heard on a phone; the sideways scroll waits for Ben's verdict.
- 🎼 Song is a playlist (2026-10-12, ADR 0024): a song tempo (➖ ➕, typed; the first beat in sets it) and 4 lanes, up to 8 (➕ Add a lane), each with a name and picture (tap for its sheet) and a speaker; a strip of 🗂️ My beats above the lanes: pick one, tap a bar on a lane for a block; tap a block for its ⟷ end, ➖ ➕, 🗑️; ↩️ Undo; keyboard on every part; ▶ Play song from bar 1 or a tapped bar number, with a playhead. Time runs across and scrolls inside the box; on a phone four lanes show and the rest scroll down. Stored as `tempo` and `lanes` on the song record, plus `slots` for older copies; old songs open as lane 1 (`web/playlist.js`, `web/song.js`; no store or backup format change). Not yet heard or tried on a phone.
- 🎹 The piano roll (2026-10-12, ADR 0023): tune rows replace melody rows, for Regular and Advanced. A tune row stores notes (`tune: [{p, s, l}]`, `web/notes.js`); its emoji (with a small 🎹) or a pill opens the roll (`web/roll.js`), ⌨️ Keys off or on: tap a square for a note, drag to stretch, tap to take out, ↩️ Undo, scale lock (♯ All notes), two octaves with ⌃ ⌄ or a swipe, the other rows as a strip, ▶ Play with a playhead, ½ Half pills with Advanced. 🎹 Add a tune beside + Add a row. Old melody rows load as notes; older copies play tune rows roughly. Grooves' bass rows load as one tune row. Not yet heard or tried on a phone.
- 🔔 Overlap (2026-10-12): on Beats a 🔔 on each row's header line (and the keys view's rows), at every level, turns the row's `ring` on or off; the sound picker no longer has it. On 🎤 Live one 🔔 Overlap right of ⏲️ Click track lets every key's hits ring over each other (up to 8), for the visit only; takes keep it (`ring` on a hit). Pads always restart. Not yet heard on a phone.
- The keys have four rows (2026-10-12): the number row, 1 … 0, above Q–P on 🎤 Live and Beats with ⌨️ Keys, alternating like bricks (Q and Z rows half a key in); by default a scale (C to high C, Ding, Ta-da). Phones get a fourth row tab; ✏️ Edit keys takes sounds and groups onto it; the ready-made layouts fill it; older saved layouts and ⭐ My keys load with it empty (ADR 0014 note). On 🔊 Pads, 1–9 still play the first nine pads. Not yet tried on a real keyboard by a kid.
- Beats with ⌨️ Keys (2026-10-12): 🆕 New stops playback and opens the scratch blank (Keys off and on, and Song); only 💾 Keep adds to 🗂️ My beats or My songs; a scratch with rows or slots it empties has ↩️ Undo; rows read emoji · pills · 🔔 · 🔊 · 🧹 · ✕; playing a key adds its row; rows show in keyboard order (stored order unchanged); no status line while recording.
- Voices are knob settings (Design 1, `docs/design/unified-sound-shaping.md`, ADR 0012): voices that move the knobs, ✦ special parts, ⤢ Turn into knobs for old sounds, ⭐ Keep as mine (synced with the person, in backups), the name in the sheet's title bar and folding sections. Knobs and Shape it are one **Layers** section: Beats-style rows in play order with 🔈 mute and ✕, + Add a layer, the kid's own steps as rows with Advanced, an ⚙️ Advanced switch at its bottom; 🪄 Voice uses the same rows. A kid's own voice can't go in a share code (postponed, decisions.md).
- Tooltips (2026-10-12): every control gets one from its `aria-label` or text (`web/tooltips.js`), unless it has a `title` of its own.
- Cloud profiles (ADR 0002, `docs/design/cloud-profiles.md`, `worker/`, `web/cloud*.js`): everyone syncs unless 📴 Offline, settings travel with the person, the PIN is asked each open (Remember / Forget on this device), share codes replace gifts (migration 001 is applied: the live checks make share codes), no families, PIN resets by email. Two devices syncing is checked on every deploy (`just cloud-e2e`); two real phones or tablets have not been tried yet.
- Cloud sync timing and the three-way merge: syncs on focus and blur and every 5 minutes while active (`web/sync-timing.js`); merges each record against its last-synced copy (`web/merge3.js`, IndexedDB `bases`); pushes carry `since`, and the Worker leaves records written after it (`stale`).
- Random person ids: every person has a random `p-` id on the device and in the cloud; the Worker makes and lists only `p-` ids. A device still linked to a plain-id profile drops the link and puts the person up as new.
- Problem reports (ADR 0009, `docs/design/automatic-logs.md`): the app side (`web/log-clean.js`, the ⚙️ Settings switch, `web/log-send.js`), the Worker's `/api/log` into D1 for 30 days, and `tools/logs.py` with its SQLite archive, fed by the `noodlepads-logs` recurrent leg (`skills/noodlepads-logs/`).
