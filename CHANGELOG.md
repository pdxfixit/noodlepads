# What's new in Noodle Pads

The version shows in the bottom corner of the app (and in ⚙️ Settings → About):
when it was last updated, like `Updated Oct 19, 9:27 PM`; About adds the exact build, a link to it on GitHub.

## 1.0 (October 2026)

The first real release. Everything here works on a phone, a tablet or a
computer, at https://noodlepads.com/.

**🎉 Play and 🛠️ Make**
- The bottom bar has two modes: 🎉 Play (🔊 Pads and 🎤 Live) and 🛠️ Make
  (🥁 Beats and 🎼 Song). One tap goes back to where you were in the other.

**Sounds**
- Tap a pad to play it; hold it to keep it looping, and loop several at once.
- Record your own sounds, or add a sound file.
- Make a sound silly with a voice: Chipmunk, Monster, Robot, Underwater, Alien,
  Backwards and more. Stack voices, tweak the knobs, and keep a mix as your own.
- Trim a sound, fade it, chop it into several pads, or 🔊 Even it out so it
  plays as loud as the ready-made ones.
- 125 ready-made sounds (145 with Advanced's sharps and flats): drums, hand
  drums, hip hop, notes, chords, bass, game, effects, animals, sci-fi, voices
  and silly ones. The drums and most animals are real recordings, and they
  all play about as loud as each other.
- 🎹 Who plays: pick the instrument for Notes, Chords and Bass, from piano,
  strings, marimba, steel drum, guitar and more (11 for Notes).
- 🎨 Pad skins: every pad as Tiles, Slam buttons, Lit pads, Switch, Spinner,
  Jelly or Googly eyes.

**Music**
- 🥁 Beats: tap the grid to make a beat, pick a tempo, and keep as many beats
  as you like. 💃 Grooves gives 51 ready-made ones in seven styles, from hip
  hop and reggae to chiptune.
- ⌨️ Keys: play sounds like a keyboard right under your beat, and ● Record
  what you play into it.
- 🎹 The piano roll: write a tune note by note.
- 🎚️ The mixer: a fader, Mute, Solo and an effect for every row of a beat.
- 🎼 Song: line up your beats on lanes into a whole song.
- 🎤 Live: play the keys or big touch pads along with a click track, keep
  your takes, and lay the keys out your way.
- 🎧 From a song: let it listen to a song (a browser tab, the microphone or a
  file) and it turns what it hears into beats.

**Everyone gets their own board**
- 👋 Who's playing? greets each new person: pick a stage name and how to play.
- Each person has a fun name, a picture, a color and their own sounds and
  beats. 🗂️ See all shows everything they have made.
- Each person has a level: 🧸 Kids (big picture pads, a mic with silly voices,
  a simple beat and toy keys, with no reading needed), Regular, or Advanced
  (more beat and sound tools).
- ☁️ The cloud: a person's board syncs, so it opens on another device too.
  Add a PIN, or switch ☁️ off to keep them on this device only.
- Share a sound or a beat with a code.

**For grown-ups**
- 💾 Save a backup of everything, or of one person, to a file, and load it on
  any device.
- ⚙️ Settings: each person's level and pad skin, sharing, backups and the data
  switches. Leaving 🧸 Kids and sending feedback ask a grown-up sum first.
- 📊 Play data (on unless you turn it off) counts what gets used; 📮 problem
  reports (off unless you turn them on) say what went wrong. Neither ever
  includes sounds or names. The Privacy policy, linked beside the version,
  says exactly what leaves the device.
- Feedback (⚙️ Settings → About) sends us a note.
- Install it from the browser like an app; after one visit it opens with no
  network.
- ❓ Help explains every button.
