# What's new in Noodle Pads

The version shows in the bottom corner of the app (and in ⚙️ Settings → About):
when it was last updated, like `Updated Oct 19, 9:27 PM`; About adds the exact build, a link to it on GitHub.

## 1.0 (October 2026)

The first real release. Everything here works on a phone, a tablet or a
computer, at https://noodlepads.com/.

**Sounds**
- Tap a pad to play it; hold it to keep it looping, and loop several at once.
- Record your own sounds, or add a sound file.
- Make a sound silly with a voice: Chipmunk, Monster, Robot, Underwater, Alien,
  Backwards and more. Stack voices, tweak the knobs, and keep a mix as your own.
- Trim a sound, fade it, or chop it into several pads.
- Over 100 ready-made sounds: drums, notes, chords, bass, game, animals and more.

**Music**
- 🥁 Beats: tap the grid to make a beat, pick a tempo, mix the rows, and keep
  as many beats as you like. 💃 Grooves gives 33 ready-made ones to start from.
- ⌨️ Keys: play sounds like a keyboard and record what you play.
- 🎼 Song: line up your beats into a whole song.
- 🎤 Live: play along with a click track.

**Everyone gets their own board**
- Each person has a fun name, a picture and their own sounds and beats.
- ☁️ The cloud (optional): put a person in the cloud to open their board on
  another device, with a PIN if you want one.
- Share a sound or a beat with a code.

**For grown-ups**
- 💾 Save a backup of everything to a file, and load it on any device.
- ⚙️ Settings: Advanced mode for more controls, sizes, and keyboard layouts.
- 📮 Problem reports are off unless you turn them on, and never include
  sounds or names. ⚙️ Settings → 🔓 What's private says exactly what leaves the device.
- ❓ Help explains every button.
