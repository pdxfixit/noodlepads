Each was made mono, 22050 Hz, 16-bit, cut to a second or two from where it
starts, and faded out (tools/make_samples.py). The number in each name is
the MIDI note it plays (60 is middle C). Credit to the original authors and
Versilian Studios is encouraged by the libraries, not required.

Every other clip in this folder is made by tools/make_placeholders.py, or
is one of the old app's starter sounds.
